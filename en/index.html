Try the POS

This is the real POS California uses at its two restaurants, with test data. Order at the kiosk and watch the ticket reach the kitchen.

Open full screen ↗

Kitchen and till ask for a PIN: in the demo any 4 digits work. The POS itself is in Spanish and English.

  • A kiosk that sells on its own. Video screensaver, Spanish and English, and a photo of every dish.
  • Every dish their way. Remove ingredients, add extras or make it a meal with fries and a drink; the price updates instantly.
  • Real-time kitchen. Orders arrive with a timer and are marked ready with one tap.
  • Till and receipts. Shifts, cash or card payments, itemised tax and automatic printing.
  • Multiple locations. Medina de Pomar and Villarcayo, each with its own kitchen and till.

How do we work?

First, a call. You tell us what you want to achieve, not which technology to use, and we send you in writing what we will build, what it costs and when it will be ready.

In the first week, design. Brand, screens and the full journey before writing code, so you see your product and can change it while it is still cheap.

Then, our own code. No templates: every animation, screen and sound is written for your project.

At launch, we stay. We put the site on your domain or the app on Google Play, measure how it is used and keep improving it with you.
